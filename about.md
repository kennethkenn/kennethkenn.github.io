      <span class="section-plate">Narrative</span>
      <h2>Curiosity first.<br>Then craft.</h2>
      <p>My journey began with a curiosity for how things work on the web. That curiosity evolved into a passion for building robust applications that don't just function well but feel great to use.</p>
      <p>I believe in the power of minimalism and clarity. Whether I'm designing a UI or architecting a database, I strive for elegance and efficiency. I mostly enjoy working with C++, Java, and Python - especially where performance, structure, and product thinking meet.</p>
      <span class="bio-signature">- Kenneth Buchunju, The Mint Coder</span>

      <div>
        <span class="section-plate">Principles</span>
        <h2 class="display">How the work<br>gets made.</h2>
      </div>
      <p class="section-lede">A small set of rules that survive deadlines, rewrites, and the temptation to reach for something clever.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="principle-list">
      <article class="principle" data-reveal style="--i:0">
        <span>01</span>
        <h3>Clarity over cleverness</h3>
        <p>The best code reads like a clear sentence. I optimise for the next person who has to understand it, including future me.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="principle" data-reveal style="--i:1">
        <span>02</span>
        <h3>Measure, then move</h3>
        <p>Performance work begins with evidence. Profilers, traces, and benchmarks - never vibes - decide where the effort goes.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="principle" data-reveal style="--i:2">
        <span>03</span>
        <h3>Finish the edges</h3>
        <p>The last ten percent is the product: empty states, error messages, motion, and the details nobody puts in a ticket.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </section>
